Our Roasting Philosophy

From High-Elevation Soil to Cup

We obsess over the science of caramelization and airflow so you don't have to.

01
🌍

Single-Estate Sourcing

We work with small cooperatives in Ethiopia, Colombia, Guatemala, and Sumatra that farm with organic and regenerative principles above 1,500m elevation.

02
🌡️

Micro-Batch Roasting

Each harvest is test-profiled using precise temperature curve logging. We never over-roast, ensuring natural terroir and delicate floral acids remain intact.

03
📦

Degassed & Sealed

Packaged in biodegradable pouches equipped with one-way aroma degassing valves to preserve peak freshness for up to 6 months.
